🐾 Filming Scripts · August 2026

What to film & say, every day

Sugar Sweet Georgia Puppies. Tap any day to open its full script. One post per weekday.

How every clip works

  1. Film vertical, 10–30 sec, clean spot with the store behind Phil.
  2. Land the hook in the first 1–2 seconds — say it first, no windup.
  3. Turn HDR video OFF (iPhone → Settings → Camera → Record Video) so the color stays true.
  4. Send the clip to Claude with the breed. It's edited, named, captioned, and posted automatically — you don't write captions.
  5. Save/name it with the filename shown so the breed in the caption is always right.
