What the research says (and how this calendar uses it)

  • Hook in ~1.5 seconds. Every day's hook is written to land instantly — no slow intros.
  • 10–18 sec clips retain best. Keep them short; one idea per clip.
  • Trust content converts. Anti-puppy-mill + vet-care days are placed every week — it's the #1 buyer objection.
  • Local + breed keywords = discovery. Hooks name breeds and Lawrenceville/Gwinnett/Atlanta on purpose.
  • National Dog Day (Aug 26) is the month's anchor — a big multi-breed celebration post.
  • Back-to-school (Gwinnett starts early Aug) + Relaxation Day (Aug 15) give timely, shareable angles.

How to film each day

  • Film vertical, 10–30 sec, clean spot with the store behind Phil.
  • Land the hook in the first 1–2 seconds — no windup.
  • Send the clip to Claude with the breed → it's edited, named, and posts itself.
  • Turn HDR video OFF in the iPhone camera (Settings → Camera → Record Video) so color stays true.

Rotate these CTAs

  • "Come meet our puppies in Lawrenceville."
  • "Call (678) 253-4081 to learn more."
  • "Text (678) 990-0775 for pricing."
  • "Visit us at 1848 Old Norcross Rd."
  • "Send this to someone looking for a small hypoallergenic puppy."

Filename = accuracy

Name each clip with the breed (that's the [breed] in each cell). The caption, hashtags, and YouTube title all generate from it — so a poodle never becomes a Maltipoo. No breed in the name still posts a clean, general caption.
